# Disclaimer

**Credence is a methodology research project and a personal toolkit. It is not
investment advice.**

- Nothing in this repository is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any
  security, and nothing here creates an advisory or fiduciary relationship.
- The scoring protocol grades the *credibility of information*, not the truth
  of predictions. An `ACT`-grade item is decision-grade **input**, never a
  signal that acting is wise or profitable.
- The ladder engine encodes one risk-management philosophy among many. It can
  and will lose money in some states of the world, including states where
  doing nothing would have been better.
- All example data in `examples/` is synthetic and illustrative.
- Past behavior of any rule, weight, or threshold in this repository — live or
  simulated — is not indicative of future results.

You are solely responsible for your own decisions. Markets carry the risk of
total loss regardless of how carefully you grade your information.
